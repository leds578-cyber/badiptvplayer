Privacy Policy

Bad IPTV Player for Samsung Smart TV · Last updated: November 7, 2026

Bad IPTV Player ("the app") is a video player. It plays playlists that you add yourself. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The app is published by Bad IPTV Player ("we").

Information stored on your TV

To play your content, the app saves the following on your TV only:

  • Playlists you add: their name, playlist address (URL), and, for Xtream Codes accounts, the server address, username and password.
  • Your settings, favorite channels and the last channel you watched.

This information stays in the app's storage on your TV. We do not receive it, and it is not sent to us or to any third party. Deleting a playlist in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Connections the app makes

The app connects only to the servers in the playlists you add, to download the playlist and to play its video streams. Those servers belong to your TV provider, not to us. Your provider's own privacy policy covers what they collect when your TV connects to them.

If you choose "Explore the demo", the app plays public test video streams from Mux, Apple and Unified Streaming.

What we do not collect

  • We have no servers that receive data from the app.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • The app does not collect personal information, location, contacts, or viewing history outside your TV.

Content

The app does not include, sell or provide any TV channels, movies or series. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to watch the content in the playlists you add.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
